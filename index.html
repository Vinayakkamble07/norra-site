Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026  ·  Norra Chrome Extension

Overview

Norra is a Chrome extension that enhances Google NotebookLM with AI chat capture, bulk URL import, notebook management, and a prompt library. This policy explains what data Norra accesses and what it does — and doesn't — do with it.

Short version: Norra never collects, stores on any server, or sells your data. Everything stays on your device or goes directly to Google's NotebookLM — using your own Google account, exactly as if you had done it manually.

Who we are

Norra is an independent Chrome extension. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Google LLC or the NotebookLM product. For privacy questions: privacy@norra.app

Where data goes

When you capture or import content, Norra sends it directly to notebooklm.google.com via NotebookLM's own internal API, authenticated with your existing Google session. No data is routed through Norra's infrastructure — there are no Norra servers in the flow.

Local storage

Norra uses chrome.storage.local to store on your device: your account email, cached notebook list, and your saved prompts. This data never leaves your device. Clear it anytime by removing the extension.

Permissions explained

  • storage — Save prompts, notebook cache, and email locally.
  • activeTab — Read the current tab when you click "Send to NotebookLM".
  • scripting — Inject the capture button and extract conversation text.
  • tabs — Find or open the NotebookLM tab when needed.
  • downloads — Save exported notebooks to your device.
  • notebooklm.google.com — Make API calls to NotebookLM on your behalf.
  • <all_urls> — Run the capture script on any site so the button appears on supported sites (ChatGPT, Claude, Reddit, etc.). The script silently does nothing on unrecognized sites.

What Norra never does

  • Never sends data to analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
  • Never reads browser history, passwords, or unrelated page content.
  • Never stores captured conversations — they pass through in memory only.
  • Never sells, shares, or monetizes user data in any form.
  • Never monitors your browsing in the background.

Children's privacy

Norra is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Contact privacy@norra.app with any concerns.

Third-party services

Norra only communicates with Google's NotebookLM. Your NotebookLM usage is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. The landing page loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Changes

Material changes will update the "Last updated" date above and may be noted in the Chrome Web Store listing.
