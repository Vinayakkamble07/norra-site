Frequently asked questions.

Which AI chat sites does Norra support?

Norra captures conversations from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and Mistral. It also scrapes threads from Reddit, Twitter/X, and LinkedIn. Any page can be saved to NotebookLM via the web capture button.

Does Norra store my chats or data anywhere?

No. Nothing is stored on Norra's servers. Captured content goes directly from your browser to your NotebookLM notebook. Your data stays yours.

How does the bulk URL import work?

Open the Norra popup, go to the Bulk Import tab, paste a list of URLs (one per line), choose a target notebook, and click Import. Norra fetches each page and adds it as a source.

Is Norra affiliated with Google or NotebookLM?

No. Norra is an independent Chrome extension built to enhance your NotebookLM workflow. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Google.
