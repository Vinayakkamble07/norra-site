Build a research workflow that actually sticks

From the Norra desk

Good research is less about collecting everything and more about creating a reliable path from curiosity to clarity. Start by capturing sources at the moment you find them, while the context is still fresh.

Keep one notebook for each meaningful question, then use Norra to send conversations, articles, and reference pages directly into it. At the end of a session, ask NotebookLM to identify the strongest evidence, open questions, and the next action worth taking.

The result: your research becomes a trail you can revisit instead of a collection of tabs you have to rediscover.

The habit compounds quickly. Once every source has a home, you stop re-reading the same article twice. You stop losing the insight you had at 11pm. And when you return to a question after a week away, NotebookLM can reconstruct your thinking from what you saved — not from memory.

Norra makes the capture step nearly frictionless: one click from an AI chat conversation, one click from a web page, or paste a list of URLs in bulk. The bottleneck is never the tool. It is the decision to capture before you move on.

Start small: pick one active question you are researching right now. Create a notebook for it. Capture your next five sources there instead of leaving them in tabs. Run one NotebookLM session at the end of the week. Notice what changes.
